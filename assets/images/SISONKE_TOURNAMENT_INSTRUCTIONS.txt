SISONKE SOCCER TOURNAMENT - PHOTO INSTRUCTIONS
===============================================

Please add your Sisonke Soccer Tournament photos to this directory.

## Recommended Photos:

1. **sisonke-main.jpg** - Main tournament photo (action shot, teams, or opening ceremony)
   - Will be displayed in the Community section
   - Recommended size: 1200x800px

2. **sisonke-banner.jpg** (Optional) - Tournament banner/logo
   - Recommended size: 800x400px

3. **sisonke-gallery/** (Optional) - Create a subfolder for multiple photos
   - Team photos
   - Action shots
   - Award ceremonies
   - Community events

## How to Add Photos:

### Option 1: Single Main Photo
1. Save your best tournament photo as: `sisonke-main.jpg`
2. Place it in this directory: `assets/images/`
3. The website will automatically display it

### Option 2: Multiple Photos (Gallery)
1. Create folder: `assets/images/sisonke-gallery/`
2. Add multiple photos with names like:
   - `action1.jpg`
   - `teams.jpg`
   - `ceremony.jpg`
   - `community.jpg`
